GITTO FX - JG Productions
=========================

Twenty plugins:
  Gitto FX EQ, Compressor, Limiter, Reverb, Delay, Chorus,
  Multiband, Smooth, Opto Comp, FET Comp, Bus Comp, De-Esser, Channel Strip,
  Saturator, Distortion, Tape, Plate, Space, Tune, Shaper.
Formats: VST3 and Audio Unit. macOS 10.13 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.

INSTALL
1. Double-click "Install Gitto FX.command".
   The first time, macOS will say it cannot verify the file. That is expected:
   these builds are not notarized with Apple yet.
     - macOS 14 and earlier: right-click the file, choose Open, then Open again.
     - macOS 15 and later: open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
       down, click "Open Anyway" next to the file's name, then run it again.
2. Open FL Studio > Options > Manage plugins > Find installed plugins.
3. The plugins appear under Effects as "Gitto FX ...".

INSTALL BY HAND (if you prefer)
  Copy the contents of the VST3 folder to   ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  Copy the contents of the AU folder to     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
  Then run this once in Terminal:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/Gitto\ FX*.vst3 ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/Gitto\ FX*.component

REMOVE
  Delete the "Gitto FX ..." items from the two folders above.
